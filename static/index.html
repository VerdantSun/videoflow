    Questions

    Which videos work?

    Videos with English or Hindi captions turned on. If YouTube blocks the fetch or captions are off, paste the transcript into the first step instead.

    Are my videos or chats saved?

    No. The server keeps nothing. The transcript and your questions are sent to Groq to produce each answer, and your browser holds the rest until you close the tab.

    What about long videos?

    The AI reads the first 20,000 characters of the transcript to stay inside free-tier limits. The Transcript tab tells you when a video was cut.

    Why did a run fail?

    The failed step turns red and the Run log tab says why. Free-tier rate limits usually clear after a minute.
